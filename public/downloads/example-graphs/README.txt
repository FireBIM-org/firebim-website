FireBIM synthetic example graphs
================================

These Turtle (.ttl) files contain fabricated buildings, rooms, values, and
relationships. They do not represent real buildings. They are intended only
for graph browsing, RuleBook development, demonstrations, and automated tests.

The examples are not evidence of regulatory compliance and must not be used as
building-design guidance.

Each country has two matching examples:

- synthetic-three-storey-office-fbo-CC.ttl
  A three-storey office with exactly 25 spaces, including a canteen and kitchen.

- synthetic-unrealistic-edge-case-fbo-CC.ttl
  A deliberately impossible one-storey graph with contradictory/extreme values.

CC is one of: be (Belgium), dk (Denmark), lt (Lithuania), nl (Netherlands), or
pt (Portugal). Each file uses only generic FBO and the corresponding national
module. No IFC assertion is included.
